Knowing algorithms and passing an interview are different skills. This roadmap is about the second: working through a problem in 45 minutes while someone watches, explaining your thinking, finding the edge cases before they find you, and stating what your solution costs.

It assumes the techniques from Data structures and algorithms; take that roadmap first, or alongside. Every lesson ends in a fresh problem, written for this roadmap, so practising the process means solving something new. The last module is two timed mock interviews: do them with a real clock.
