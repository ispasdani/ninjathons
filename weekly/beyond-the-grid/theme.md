Grids are graphs with the edges drawn for you. This week the edges come as a list, and you decide how to store and walk them: **union-find** for groups, a **topological order** for prerequisites, **Dijkstra** for the fastest route, and a **depth-first search** that remembers how far back each part of the graph can reach.

Expect repeated links, loops, disconnected pieces, and chains 100,000 long, deep enough to overflow a recursive search.
