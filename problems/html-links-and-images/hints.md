## Hint

A picture is an `<img>` element. It has no closing tag: everything goes in attributes, `<img src="…" alt="…" width="240" height="160">`.

## Hint

A link is an `<a>` element: the text between the tags is what people click, and `href` is where it goes. It sits inside the paragraph, after the sentence.

## Hint

The `alt` text is what screen readers say and what shows if the picture can't load, so it should describe the picture.
