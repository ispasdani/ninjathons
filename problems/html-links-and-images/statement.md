Write the HTML for a short page about a cat, so it matches the target. The CSS is given.

## What to build

1. A main heading: **Our cat, Pixel**
2. A picture of her, with the address given in the starter file, **240 pixels wide and 160 high**, and the alternative text **A grey cat asleep on a keyboard**.
3. A paragraph: **Pixel came to us in 2024.** followed by a link that says **Read her adoption story** and goes to `https://example.com/pixel`.

The checks look at each element's text and at the attributes that matter: the picture's `src`, `alt`, `width` and `height`, and the link's `href`.
