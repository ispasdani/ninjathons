Return the average (the mean) of the numbers in `nums`: their sum divided by how many there are. For an empty list, return `0`.

The average doesn't have to be a whole number: the average of `[1, 2]` is `1.5`. Answers within `0.000001` of the exact value are accepted.

## Constraints

- `0 <= nums.length <= 100000`
- `-10^9 <= nums[i] <= 10^9`
