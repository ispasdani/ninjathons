## Hint

Going to whichever meeting starts first can lock you into one long meeting while three short ones pass by.

## Hint

Think about your first meeting. Whatever the best plan is, swapping its first meeting for the one that **ends** earliest never makes it worse: you're free sooner.

## Hint

Sort by end time and go through the meetings, taking each one that starts at or after the end of the last one you took. Comparing against every earlier meeting (a dynamic program) works too, but it's O(n²).
