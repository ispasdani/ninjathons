You've been invited to `meetings`, each `[start, end]` running from minute `start` up to minute `end`. You can only be in one meeting at a time, and you stay for the whole of any meeting you go to. A meeting may start at the minute the previous one ends.

Return the largest number of meetings you can attend.

## Constraints

- `1 <= meetings.length <= 100000`
- `0 <= start < end <= 10^9`
