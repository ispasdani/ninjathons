Given a string `s` made of the characters `(`, `)`, `[`, `]`, `{` and `}`, return `true` if the brackets are balanced:

- every opening bracket is closed by a bracket of the same type,
- brackets close in the right order (the most recently opened one first),
- every closing bracket has an opening bracket before it.

## Constraints

- `1 <= s.length <= 100000`
