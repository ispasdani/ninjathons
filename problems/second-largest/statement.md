Return the second largest **different** value in `nums`: the largest value that is smaller than the maximum. If every number is the same, there's no such value: return `-1`.

In `[4, 9, 9, 2]` the largest is 9 and the second largest is 4, not the other 9.

## Constraints

- `1 <= nums.length <= 100000`
- `0 <= nums[i] <= 10^9`
