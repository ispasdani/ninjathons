The HTML for a profile card is given. Write the CSS that makes it look like the target, at both widths the checks use: 800 pixels and a small 320-pixel phone.

## What to match

- The page background is `#f5f5f5`.
- The card is **white**, **320 pixels wide** (but never wider than the page allows), **centred** horizontally, with **24 pixels of padding**, **12-pixel rounded corners**, and a 1-pixel `#e5e5e5` border.
- Inside, the avatar and the text sit **side by side** with **16 pixels** between them, vertically centred. The avatar is a **56-pixel circle** filled `#c4f012`.
- The name is 18 pixels and weight 600; the role under it is 14 pixels in `#737373`.

Use *Overlay* to lay the target over your page: anything out of place shows up doubled.
