Count how often each word appears in a text.

A **word** is a run of English letters (`a`–`z`, `A`–`Z`); anything else separates words. Words are compared without regard to case: `The` and `the` are the same word.

## Input

Some text over one or more lines, up to the end of the input.

## Output

For each distinct word, print the word in lower case, a space, and how many times it appears, one per line. Order the lines by count, highest first; break ties alphabetically. Print nothing if the text has no words.

## Constraints

- The input is at most 1,000,000 characters of printable ASCII and newlines.
