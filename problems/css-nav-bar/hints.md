## Hint

`display: flex` on the `nav` puts the name and the list side by side. `justify-content: space-between` pushes them to opposite ends, and `align-items: center` centres them vertically.

## Hint

A `ul` comes with bullets, a margin and a left padding. `list-style: none; margin: 0; padding: 0;` removes all three.

## Hint

The list itself needs `display: flex` and `gap: 24px` to lay the links out in a row. Links are underlined until you set `text-decoration: none`.
