You have a stack of books; book `i` has `pages[i]` pages. You read at a steady speed of `k` pages an hour, and each hour you work on one book only: you read `k` pages of it, or finish it if fewer are left and rest for the rest of that hour.

Return the smallest whole speed `k` that lets you finish every book within `hours` hours.

## Constraints

- `1 <= pages.length <= 100000`
- `1 <= pages[i] <= 10^9`
- `pages.length <= hours <= 10^9`
