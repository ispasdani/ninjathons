`board` is a partly filled 9 × 9 Sudoku grid, one string of 9 characters per row: a digit `1` to `9`, or `.` for an empty cell.

Return `true` if no rule is broken **so far**: no digit appears twice in any row, in any column, or in any of the nine 3 × 3 boxes. Empty cells don't matter, and the board doesn't have to be solvable.

## Constraints

- `board` has 9 rows of 9 characters, each `1`–`9` or `.`
