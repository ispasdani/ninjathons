A row of houses holds `houses[i]` coins each. A thief can take the coins from any houses they like, except two next to each other: neighbouring houses share an alarm.

Return the most coins the thief can take.

The answer can be larger than a 32-bit integer.

## Constraints

- `1 <= houses.length <= 100000`
- `0 <= houses[i] <= 10^9`
